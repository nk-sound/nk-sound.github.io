BIO

Hi I'm Nico, and I'm a sound designer on Vancouver Island, Canada.

I currently work as a freelancer in games and film.

I started working in digital audio in 2011 in Sydney, Australia while I was studying an undergrad in Philosophy & Religion.

Though it began as a hobby it quickly overtook my attention and free time and soon enough I began working in a professional capacity.

After many years I have a accrued a range of work experience across game design, post production for film & tv, live sound, and commercial music production. I've found these experiences to inform one another—each offering unique lessons and strategies for telling stories with sound.

These days I enjoy spending time designing audio systems in FMOD, Unreal Engine, Wwise, and Unity.

Tools

Marker Spotter

A REAPER Lua tool for film spotting sessions. Scans project markers, matches them against user-defined rules, and commits MIDI notes to specified tracks automatically — with a rule-card UI, a live mini-timeline, and saveable/loadable presets.

Take n Bake

A REAPER Lua script that bakes Take FX onto media items while preserving the full original source file length, so item handles can still be pulled out to the original file's boundaries.
